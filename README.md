# lucerna-cast

The TV page for Lucerna, an Android browser that finds the video on a web page and plays it on your TV. When Lucerna
casts to a Chromecast or a TV with Google Cast built in, the TV loads this page (a Google Cast Web Receiver). The phone
sends the video's address and what to show; the video comes from the phone over your home network or from the site.
The page loads only Google's Cast receiver library and its own files: no analytics, nothing stored.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Sans, and IBM Plex Mono, under the SIL Open Font License (texts in fonts/).

Published from Lucerna's source (commit 4227420); changes made here are overwritten.
